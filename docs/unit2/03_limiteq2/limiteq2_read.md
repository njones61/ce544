# Pre-Class - The Limit Equilibrium Method, Part 2

This lecture covers the methods that work on surfaces of any shape: simplified Janbu and its correction factor, the force equilibrium procedures of the Corps of Engineers and Lowe and Karafiath, and the two complete equilibrium methods, Spencer's and Morgenstern–Price. Each method is run on the example slope from Part 1, and the lecture closes by comparing the seven methods XSLOPE supports and how to choose among them.
