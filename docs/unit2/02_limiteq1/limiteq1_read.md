# Pre-Class - The Limit Equilibrium Method, Part 1

Limit equilibrium compares the shear strength along a chosen slip surface with the shear stress needed to hold the soil above it in equilibrium, and their ratio is the factor of safety. This lecture sets up the method and works through the infinite slope (with seepage and submerged slopes), the φ = 0 circle, the method of slices and its unknowns and equations, the general method of slices, the Ordinary Method of Slices, and Bishop's simplified method. OMS and Bishop are run on an example slope from XSLOPE, which the next lecture uses for every other method.
