# Homework - Shear Strength for Slope Analysis

A 5 m embankment of compacted sandy gravel will be built in about four months on a clay foundation. The water table is 1 m below the ground surface. The clay's unit weight is 17 kN/m³ above the water table and 17.5 kN/m³ below it. CD tests on the fill gave $c'$ = 0 and $\phi'$ = 36°. Your job is to reduce the test results on the clay and choose the strengths for the stability analyses. Put your solution in a Word or Excel document and show all work. Draw Mohr circles to scale, with the same scale on both axes.

## Problem 1 - CU Tests

Three CU tests with pore pressures measured were run on samples of the clay. At failure:

| Test | Cell pressure $\sigma_3$ (kPa) | Deviator stress $\sigma_1 - \sigma_3$ (kPa) | Pore pressure $u_f$ (kPa) |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| A | 75 | 89 | 30 |
| B | 150 | 148 | 68 |
| C | 300 | 264 | 142 |

1. Draw the total and effective stress circles at failure.
2. Determine $c'$ and $\phi'$ from the effective circles.
3. Determine $c_{cu}$ and $\phi_{cu}$ from the total circles.

## Problem 2 - Undrained Strength with Depth

UU tests on samples from a boring gave these deviator stresses at failure:

| Depth (m) | 2 | 5 | 8 | 11 | 14 |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| $\sigma_1 - \sigma_3$ at failure (kPa) | 44 | 26 | 36 | 50 | 59 |

1. Compute $s_u$ at each depth and plot $s_u$ against depth.
2. Compute $\sigma'_v$ and $s_u/\sigma'_v$ at each depth. Which samples behave like a normally consolidated clay? What explains the sample at 2 m?
3. Describe the undrained strength for an analysis: the strength of the upper layer, and below it a value at the top of the layer plus a rate of increase with depth.

## Problem 3 - Strengths for the Analyses

1. For the end of construction, give the strength parameters you would use for the fill and for each part of the clay, and explain why. How are the pore pressures in the clay accounted for?
2. Do the same for the long term. Where do the pore pressures come from?
3. Which of the two conditions do you expect to be more critical for this embankment? Why?

## Problem 4 - A Slope Failure

Choose a slope failure that we did not cover in class and write a one-page summary. Include:

- where and when it happened, and how large it was,
- its causes, sorted into those that decreased the strength and those that increased the shear stress,
- which analysis condition (end of construction, long term, rapid drawdown, or earthquake) you would use to analyze it, and which strengths, and
