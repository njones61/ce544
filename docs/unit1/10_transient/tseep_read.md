# Pre-Class - Transient Seepage

Every seepage problem so far has been steady: the boundary conditions stay put and the heads settle to one answer. In a transient problem the boundary conditions change with time, a reservoir drawn down or a river in flood, and the heads change with them. The soil stores and releases water as the heads change, so a transient analysis needs two more soil properties, specific storage and specific yield, and a schedule for the boundary conditions.
